# v0.12.0 — Case-first continuous contributions

**Class:** prospective **MINOR** on the 0.x line. Predecessor: [upstream-old v0.11.0 package](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream-old/blob/main/changes/v0.11.0.md), [pre-split observed main 5e641ec](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream-old/commit/5e641ec9401b40800080791b2e490491522b1734).

## New contract

- One independently useful improvement at an original target repository is one durable **Upstream issue**, not an assessment batch, per-chat iteration or recurring revisit. Unrelated opportunities may use separate issues. A session may end while the issue continues.
- The issue holds product need, dated hypothesis provenance, source-native evidence, fork/PR links, outcome and next decision.
- The correctly parented **[opensiro-contributions](https://github.com/opensiro-contributions)** fork holds real target code, working branch and native tests; external maintainer owns the eventual original-project merge.
- The existing **[vsm-oss-organization](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization)** owns shared issue selection, S2–S5 authority/coordination/observability and Organization metric aggregation. No cloned VSM, scheduler or new KPI.
- Retain capability-first need/counterfactual, honest source-first versus strictly assessment-derived origin, independent native behavior validation, legal and fork preflight.
- Preserve predecessor v0.10.0 **explicit current-chat approval for exact external public action/content** and v0.11.0 **early working-fork verification**. The user can permit internal documentation changes without granting external maintainer outreach.

## Incompatibility and evidence

Legacy frozen batch files and prior case PRs remain in [upstream-old](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream-old) with their original links and historical versions; they are not silently converted to new issues or regraded. Old scripts/registries and accepted-outcome exporter have **not** been migrated. Until a validated new export and an atomic Organization source-contract change exists, new Upstream cannot claim a live `verified_external_outcomes` KPI. [Migration](../docs/MIGRATION.md).

The documentation and templates introduced here do **not** constitute evidence of autonomous S3/S4 function, a live execution agent, target CI parity, third-party merge or deployed product benefit.
