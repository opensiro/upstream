# v0.13.0 — Fork-local history, derived Upstream

**Prospective MINOR; 2026-11-15.**

## Approved change
- The source of truth for detailed **per-harness attention**, **per-improvement lifecycle**, and **attributed significant events/decisions** moves to `.opensiro/` on a dedicated `opensiro/meta` branch of a verified target-specific `opensiro-contributions` fork.
- Upstream remains a lean **read-only aggregate, human operating entry and exceptional pre-fork intake**. A new issue is not mandatory for every improvement after the fork owns its operational data. No duplicated giant Upstream transaction ledger or per-batch case registry.
- The source of truth for external PR merge/review status remains the **original third-party GitHub repository**, not the snapshot in a fork or in Upstream.
- Attention modes `ACTIVE`, `WATCHING`, `DORMANT`, `ARCHIVED` are discretionary; **no mandatory maintenance TTL or implied SLA**. Human `PAUSE/STOP/RESUME` must be attributable and observed by cooperating executors before new effectful work.
- Parallel historical migration uses **one verified fork as the work shard**, with reviewed inventory/progress, stable intervention identity and conflict checks: [programme #2](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream/issues/2).

## Preserved
Source-native experiments and evidence, exact initial Index chronology, legal/CLA/DCO checks, per-action third-party-publication consent, old frozen sources, Organization S1–S5 decisions and existing metric authority. The protocol bump grants **no new automation, fork-writing rights, website synchronization or verified product-usefulness claim**.

## Not yet implemented
Fork-local files on real contribution forks; a strict metadata validator, per-fork migration executor, source-fresh observer/aggregate and Organization collector cutover. #2 tracks work and receipts. Do not report their completion merely from documentation or the v0.13.0 number.

## Compatibility
v0.12.0 long-lived Upstream cases remain valid historical records; new case links can remain minimal while their detailed active state eventually migrates to verified fork metadata. No retroactive reinterpretation of `upstream-old` issues/PRs.
