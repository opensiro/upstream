# Research proposal: VSM-guided autonomous evolution of third-party harness capabilities

**Status:** proposed, non-executable research protocol. **Owner:** [Upstream #56](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream/issues/56) (candidate research), not a new command, scheduled job, mandate, or assessment procedure. **Date:** 2026-11-14 UTC.

## 1. Research question and non-goals

**Question:** Can an OpenSiro VSM-guided improvement organization independently *discover, choose, implement, verify, and learn from useful new capabilities* for third-party AI harnesses **better than a comparable ordinary agentic coding/contribution pipeline**?

A bug fix, PR, green CI, source-path inspection, or VSM ownership classification alone does not answer this question. The object under study is the **OpenSiro improvement process at its own organizational boundary**. Each external harness remains a distinct technical system-in-focus with its own [canonical Index assessment](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-index). Do not treat a target's missing S3/S4/S5, low autonomy grade, or grade change as proof of product need or of OpenSiro's autonomy.

Hypotheses to test prospectively, not results:

- **H1 — opportunity quality:** VSM-guided external sensing and function/ownership mapping generate a larger share of *independently confirmed and useful* new-capability opportunities at comparable search cost than issue-first/generic feature hunting.
- **H2 — autonomous closure:** a VSM-guided organization completes a larger share of genuinely eligible improvement decisions *within pre-existing delegated authority*, while detecting conflicts, unsupported assumptions, policy exceptions and necessary escalations more reliably.
- **H3 — learning:** independently grounded maintainer/native outcomes change later candidate selection or resource decisions, rather than merely appearing in a dashboard.
- **Nulls:** VSM adds only labeling, documentation, context volume, cost or blocking; capabilities are already present; external demand is not substantiated; the ordinary agent does just as well.

Do **not** create an autonomy score, an A/C/P maturity ordering, a feature quota, or a new general capability benchmark. General per-function capability evidence has a different experimental owner, [vsm-harness-capability](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-capability).

## 2. Two recursions, six real decisions

Here the prospective **improvement organization** is the system-in-focus. Target harness assessment evidence may *inform* the process but cannot appoint organizational decision owners.

| VSM function at the OpenSiro improvement-organization boundary | Concrete decision/return to observe, not an agent name | Unproven boundary to keep visible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S1 operations** | Upstream Contributors select and exercise a native capability change in a scoped external fork, return before/after evidence and authentic upstream feedback | A source patch or issue comment is not independent operational closure or target adoption |
| **S2 coordination** | A genuine conflict between operational units (for example Index assessment drift versus an Upstream patch premise, or competing fork claims) changes one unit's scope/work | A queue, reservation file or worker handoff alone is not autonomous S2 ownership |
| **S3 inside-and-now** | An authorized owner compares current candidate commitments, time/capacity, evidence blockers and competing work, then reassigns/pauses/reprioritizes with a traceable returned effect | Static batch sort or budget enforcement is not autonomous present-control discretion |
| **S3* complementary audit** | Evidence from the original source/dependency and an independent runner challenges a primary contributor claim, and a decisive owner changes the disposition | The same agent marking its own test green, a mock-only dependency error, or a status dashboard is not complementary audit |
| **S4 outside-and-then** | Maintainer/user demand, upstream evolution, future constraints or a new product workflow are translated into competing adaptation options; an actual S3 decision then changes present implementation priorities/capabilities | Reading GitHub issues, brainstorming features, model memories or an Index grade alone is not S4 closure |
| **S5 identity/policy** | A real policy/delegation/identity conflict reaches its legitimate parent and its decision constrains later operations | Routine PR authorization is not automatically S5; CLA/DCO, third-party acceptance and maintainer authority cannot be impersonated |

Normative meanings and owner/closure tests remain in [Profile](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-profile/blob/main/PROFILE.md). The Organization [roadmap](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md) treats autonomous S3/S2/S4 as construction goals, not established permissions or grades; particularly **M5/S4 is prospective**. Current [S1 and S5 contracts](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-oss-organization/blob/main/contracts/s1/domain-contracts.md) still govern action rights.

**Architectural test:** when a new feature idea emerges, record the external signal → alternative options (S4) → live prioritization/authority decision (S3) → scoped change (S1) → independent contradiction or validation (S3*) → next decision influenced by the outcome. Include S2 only when there is a real inter-S1 disturbance, and S5 only for a legitimate reserved matter. Missing links must be `NOT_OBSERVED`, never filled with simulated role names.

## 3. First empirical baseline and its limits

The existing [27–34 quality census](../reports/2026-11-13-assessment-hypothesis-quality-baseline.md) covers 40 frozen screening rows: 23 closed-no-change, 16 evidence-blocked, one awaiting-external; recorded origin labels are 3 derived and 37 insufficient. The census **does not verify** chronological preregistration, independent native benefit, maintainer adoption or comparative VSM superiority. Its selected and overlapping cohort is not a random causal benchmark.

[Batch 39's dated branch report](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream/blob/research/upstream-upgrade-batch39-six-probes-20261114/data/assessment-candidate-batches/2026-11-14-39-SUMMARY.md) records 10 sources and 60 bounded original-file probes, not 60 runtime tests: one proposed feature already exists and nine primary opportunities lack sufficient native need/counterfactual evidence. Retain the frozen cohort as it is. Do not mine its readbacks and retroactively label an issue-driven fix assessment-derived.

These results motivate **more decisive first-party feature demand and better opportunity selection**, not relaxed admission or unbounded agent execution.

## 4. Three candidate mechanisms for an initial study

These are *test designs*, not qualified interventions, open PR instructions, or proof of demand.

1. **S4-led capability proposal — [Akmatori post-incident memory #67](https://github.com/akmatori/akmatori/issues/67).** First-party follow-ups explicitly distinguish cross-incident corroboration from outcome-backed verification. Let S4 form alternatives for how verified outcomes may safely influence future incident-memory reuse; have S3 choose among them using maintainer constraints and resource budget. Require an observable new supported memory-review workflow and native original-code before/after. This is **source-first** and cannot be credited as an independently Index-predicted issue.
2. **Index-first function-led hypothesis — [AgentBox Upstream #61](https://github.com/opensiro/upstream/issues/61).** Start from a fresh canonical assessment, pre-register a specific manager/worker overlap/feedback capability prediction *before* current original implementation/issues, then test both competing-write and disjoint-work scenarios. Check product need and maintainer design fit. The current issue calls this a candidate, not a qualified fix.
3. **Negative control for superficial VSM attribution — [AI-Scientist-v3 #5](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v3/issues/5).** A first-party issue describes baseline-evaluator nondeterminism. A conventional native bug-fix pipeline can plausibly address it; citing S3* afterward would not make it VSM-derived. Use it to test whether the VSM arm avoids attribution inflation, validates real dependency behavior and makes a justified decision *not* to add unnecessary architecture. Do not count this bug fix toward feature-generation advantage.

Use a broader source-disjoint set for any actual comparison; these three examples are *mechanism probes*, not a statistically powered A/B.

## 5. Prospective comparison against an ordinary agent pipeline

**Baseline A:** generic source-first improvement agent with the same original-code access, first-party docs, issue/PR visibility, coding/testing tools, time/token/compute budget, and permitted action rights, but no mandatory VSM ownership/closure analysis.

**Candidate B:** VSM-guided improvement organization using the **same evidence and resources** plus explicit function/decision-owner mapping, external opportunity options, present-control allocation, source-independent audit and returned feedback. Charge its additional analysis and coordination costs rather than hiding them.

Before experiments, freeze source identities/revisions, feature/problem envelopes, task allocation, target-native validation oracle, measured outcome definitions and independence/contamination rules. Prefer independently created, matched task pairs or randomized, source-disjoint allocation; do not run two agents against each other's mutable target branches. Blind the assessor of actual product benefit to arm identity when feasible. An existing maintainer issue in an arm must remain **source-first**, regardless of later VSM explanation.

**Minimum per-opportunity evidence:** frozen original SHA/license and target scope; first-party user/maintainer/functional-need signal or independently reproduced missing workflow; two plausible options including `do nothing`; predicted native effect and falsifier; four need/mechanism/counterfactual/scope gates; who selected the priority; who could exercise each decision; exact matched native before/after independent-runner receipt (or truthful blocker); adverse/regression checks; final no-benefit/benefit/inconclusive; external PR/maintainer outcome when real; and a specific later selection/control decision changed by that outcome.

Measure **separately**:

- Native product value: independently qualified new-capability cases per selected and inspected source, verified functional delta on genuine product paths, null/negative outcomes and regressions. Keep feature and repair denominators separate.
- Decision autonomy: proportion of *eligible decision opportunities* actually closed by the permitted agent owner without an ad hoc human decision; recovery from invalid hypotheses, reallocation after negative returns, and correct escalation for reserved authority. Count legitimate human-parent policy decisions as valid governance, not a failed zero-human target.
- Evidence quality: mock-only false positives, duplicated existing features, target API semantics mistakes, stale-base or license blocks recognized before implementation, and independent audits that actually alter outcomes.
- Efficiency and ecosystem feedback: compute/token time, lead-to-tested-capability latency, source-native CI, external PR lifecycle and later demonstrated benefit. A merged PR is not deployed value; absence of a PR is not automatically a failure.
- Causal and safety interpretation: same-model prior knowledge leakage, source selection bias, task difficulty, reused historical fixes, inaccessible runners, CLA/DCO and maintainer design gates.

The key outcome is **useful verified capability plus accountable autonomous decision/feedback closure**, not commits, throughput, VSM regrades or number of checked functions.

## 6. Execution boundaries and stop rule

**Phase 0 — now, under existing #56 scope:** read-only retrospective audit of candidate signals, six-function dispositions, decision-owner transitions and barriers. Reuse existing batch artifacts, [quality census](../scripts/assessment_hypothesis_quality.py) and exact outcome records. Propose prospective falsifiers and controls; no additional runtime privileges.

**Phase 1 — within current technical validation scope:** first-party deterministic scripted tests for source-native feature/workflow need and before/after effects, only *after* the four fit gates pass and with a distinct authorized runner for a strict derived claim. Respect [assessment-to-improvement constraints](ASSESSMENT_TO_IMPROVEMENT.md), native CI policy, actual dependency APIs, license and maintainer design rights. **Do not** run live model agents, provider calls, Browser quality trials or human-user tests under #56.

**Phase 2 — separate prospective organizational autonomy study, not activated by this document:** observing whether an actual agent owns S3/S4 decisions and learns over repeated returns may require an instrumented, expressly approved live organizational trial. Its boundary, authority, cost, independent audit, actor provenance, evidence/privacy constraints and stopping conditions must be decided under the owning Organization S1/S3/S5 contracts first. An offline scripted fixture may test controls but cannot establish real agent-owned autonomy. This proposal is **not** that authorization.

**Stop or downgrade a claim** if the feature is already present, the first-party need is unsubstantiated, the true third-party API cannot produce the alleged behavior, license/policy rights are absent, independent runner evidence is missing, the owner of a VSM decision is unobserved, or human/maintainer authority would be impersonated. Negative cases and correct refusals are first-class results.

## 7. What would constitute research progress?

A reviewable **first sample** is two or three independently justified *new-capability* opportunities with full pre/post native evidence plus an explicit negative control, a decision/return log for the OpenSiro operating boundary, and a cost-matched ordinary-agent comparator. No claim of VSM superiority until an actual prospective comparison shows it with acknowledged uncertainty; no claim of autonomous S4 until genuine externally oriented option development and returned S3/S1 effects are evidenced.

No new workflow, dataset schema, control-plane command, external repo write, grading system, scheduled watch, VSM semantics or autonomy mandate is introduced by this research proposal.
