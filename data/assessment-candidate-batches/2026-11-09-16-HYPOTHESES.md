## 1. smellak/gifford — finite conversation budget across versions
- **Source:** [Index S2](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-index/blob/46219bf103b0b122d86abc3ca70ae3d8bc820e2c/assessments/gi/gifford.md), reviewed external `b618cff69f3f83a4513304c55ef34fd13bfd50f5`.
- **Assessment-origin finding:** deterministic, versioned pair-loop admission caps traffic, pauses on limit and requires operator budget extension; no autonomous S2 budget owner.
- **Predicted native weakness:** two in-flight deliveries or a reply retaining a stale budget generation may bypass a pause or consume the freshly extended budget without matching generation, allowing over-limit reentry. This is specifically a suspected edge of the established feedback/closure relation, not a wish to add semantic agent negotiation.
- **Expected counterfactual:** a narrow atomic generation/paused-state gate at the genuine delivery path rejects a stale continuation while independently authorized new-generation continuation remains possible.
- **Null/falsifier:** source-native admission already uses generation CAS/lock on every delivery and its tests cover the stale/pause race; then `CLOSED_NO_CHANGE`. Do not infer weakness from the `C` label alone.
- **Native test question:** exercise overlapping deterministic deliveries/pause/extension against the actual first-party channel storage, with one nonconflicting continuation control. No live agent.

## 3. turri-oss/turri — failure-safe module lane release
