## 1. kushagrathisside/bridle — recovery after interrupted project-run admission

- **Assessment:** [Bridle](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-index/blob/46219bf103b0b122d86abc3ca70ae3d8bc820e2c/assessments/ku/kushagrathisside-bridle.md), reviewed source `9b9b9f868c18958a921b6967fcd7d64acde877fc`, `S2=C`.
- **Observed S2:** atomic board claims and duplicate issue keys coordinate PM/engineering/QA turns; a whole-project RunGuard excludes overlapping runs. Model actors do not own its arbitration rules.
- **Prediction:** a supported abrupt process termination at the transition between project RunGuard admission, board claim and worker start may leave a stale durable ownership signal that blocks a safe next run or misreports a live owner.
- **Expected native benefit if confirmed:** recover an abandoned admission without permitting simultaneous ownership of the same project or issue.
- **Null/falsifier:** operating-system lock release and existing run recovery already handle the supported abrupt-exit boundary; or recovery without owner identity cannot safely distinguish a live peer.
- **First-party test idea:** crash a deterministic local runner at documented admission transitions, retry, assert exclusive owner and successful recovery; no agent model call.

## 2. jirathip-dev/herdsman — collision preview invalidation after lane-head changes

- **Assessment:** [herdsman](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-index/blob/46219bf103b0b122d86abc3ca70ae3d8bc820e2c/assessments/he/herdsman.md), source `efd970e817c2021439c5415739e695f4af119b1d`, `S2=C`.
- **Observed S2:** a `git merge-tree` conflict preview and cached head-pair observations inform parallel issue worktrees, serial merge ordering and bounded integration update rounds.
- **Prediction:** a lane rebase or integration-head advance may leave a *cached* old-head collision verdict in the path used to decide the next warning/merge order; a safe new head could be delayed, or new overlap may not be reported.
- **Expected native benefit if confirmed:** bind every preview used for ordering/feedback to the exact head pair and recompute on drift.
- **Null/falsifier:** cache is strictly keyed by both exact head commits and every consumer re-reads the pair; or a preview is expressly advisory and never trusted as a current gate.
- **First-party test idea:** small deterministic local Git worktrees, change one head between two previews, compare fresh merge-tree outcome to returned coordination feedback.

## 3. RogyMD/onethread — resource-lease takeover after worker death

- **Assessment:** [onethread](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-index/blob/46219bf103b0b122d86abc3ca70ae3d8bc820e2c/assessments/on/onethread.md), source `27779473556ec490a03f07aaccca9ca3c1834316`, `S2=C`.
- **Observed S2:** SQLite `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` command-class leases, PID/start liveness, wait queue and PATH shims prevent expensive Xcode/simulator commands from colliding among workers.
- **Prediction:** if a worker dies while holding a class lease, an otherwise healthy waiting worker may remain blocked after the dead process is detectable, because lease reaping is not performed on a supported subsequent admission path.
- **Expected native benefit if confirmed:** safely reclaim truly dead-process leases without evicting a current PID-reused holder or bypassing capacity limits.
- **Null/falsifier:** current acquisition/reaper already verifies PID/start and makes progress on death; arbitrary commands bypassing shims are explicitly outside scope.
- **First-party test idea:** two deterministic local child processes invoke a classified shim, terminate the holder, verify wait/retry and single occupancy, with PID-start safety control.

## 5. sisyphuslabs/dori-superagent — mailbox retention after deferred-send failure

- **Assessment:** [Dori Superagent](https://github.com/opensiro/vsm-harness-index/blob/46219bf103b0b122d86abc3ca70ae3d8bc820e2c/assessments/do/dori-superagent.md), source `06c4600b77f6e81ed9bd5b706d736bec07cf27b1`, `S2=C`.
- **Observed S2:** a persistent per-pane mailbox defers cross-lane messages until the herdr/screen guard reports a recipient idle, with duty-loop flush and lost-pane feedback.
- **Prediction:** a transient transport failure during idle `flushInbox()` may remove messages from the durable mailbox before receiving a successful send acknowledgement, losing a coordination return to the intended S1 lane.
- **Expected native benefit if confirmed:** retain/requeue failed deliveries while avoiding repeated sends after success where the transport permits attribution.
- **Null/falsifier:** the first-party path clears only after confirmed success or persists a recoverable attempt; or no supported deterministic transport failure can exercise the relevant logic.
- **First-party test idea:** first-party `send`/`flushInbox` with a deterministic failing-then-successful transport stub and persistent inbox inspection; no model/PTY quality experiment.
